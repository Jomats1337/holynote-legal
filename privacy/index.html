Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers HolyNote on iOS and Android. Your personal library stays on your device unless you request online processing, such as creating an AI note or Scripture study. Separate technical, subscription, and measurement information is processed as described below; the integrations differ between platforms.

Important privacy summary
  • HolyNote currently works without a user account or cloud note sync.
  • Your saved notes, recordings, images, chat history, reading preferences, and Today progress are stored locally on your device.
  • Audio, reviewed Scripture text, YouTube links, and questions are sent only when you request an online AI feature.
  • Religious reflections, prayers, and sermons can reveal sensitive beliefs. Please share only content you are comfortable processing online.
  • The Android app sends installation, session, and selected commercial interaction events to Meta, and Adapty shares subscription lifecycle events with Meta. These integrations use pseudonymous identifiers, not the content of your library or AI requests.
  • Android also uses AppsFlyer for install and session attribution. Its app-instance identifier and limited campaign information are linked to your Adapty profile; IP addresses may be used to infer approximate location.
  • Android crash reporting through Firebase Crashlytics is optional and controlled by the separate “Crash reports” setting.
  • We do not sell personal data or show third-party advertising.

2. Information stored on your device

HolyNote stores the following information locally so the app can work:

  • notes, folders, transcripts, generated summaries, Scripture studies, flashcards, and reflections;
  • recorded or imported audio and any images you attach to a note;
  • Bible chat history, highlights, personal verse notes, reading position, and display preferences;
  • Today completion, streak progress, onboarding choices, and app settings.

This local information is not automatically synced to a HolyNote account. You can delete notes and conversations inside the app. Removing the app or erasing its data generally removes local information. On iOS, copies may remain in device backups controlled by you or Apple. Android app backup and device transfer are disabled. Deleting local data does not automatically delete records held by our service providers.

5. AI processing and sensitive content

HolyNote uses OpenAI API services to transcribe audio and generate requested notes, studies, and answers. We configure supported Responses API requests with storage disabled. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. OpenAI may retain API content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days unless a different approved retention control applies or longer retention is legally required. See OpenAI’s API privacy information.

Sermons, prayer requests, religious beliefs, names, voices, and personal stories can be sensitive. Avoid including information you do not have the right to share. AI output may be inaccurate or incomplete; verify Scripture wording and important conclusions before relying on or sharing them.

6. Service providers

Depending on the app version and the features you use, we may use the following processors:

  • OpenAI — audio transcription and generation of requested AI notes, studies, and answers.
  • Apple — App Store distribution, payments, subscription management, device permissions, and optional notification services.
  • Google Play — Android distribution, payments, and subscription management. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Adapty — subscription status, paywall delivery, purchase analytics, and an anonymous app profile. In HolyNote’s current iOS configuration, collection of the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) and IP address by the Adapty SDK is disabled. Adapty may process purchase history, app-instance or other device identifiers, device model, operating system, language, and paywall interactions. See Adapty’s End-Users Privacy Policy.
  • AppsFlyer (iOS) — installation attribution and measurement of selected app or subscription events. Depending on configuration and consent, it may process an AppsFlyer identifier, IP address, device and app information, install source, and event data. See AppsFlyer’s Privacy Policy.
  • AppsFlyer (Android) — install and session attribution, campaign measurement, and fraud prevention. It may process a pseudonymous AppsFlyer app-instance identifier, IP address and inferred approximate location, device and app technical information, launch timing, install-referrer and campaign information. Its identifier and limited attribution fields are passed to Adapty. Configured Meta partner sharing is enabled for installation and attribution measurement; AppsFlyer-to-Meta in-app event postbacks are disabled. See AppsFlyer’s Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Android) — installation and app-event measurement, advertising attribution, campaign optimization, and subscription measurement. Meta receives the Facebook SDK app-instance identifier, app and device technical information, event times, selected interaction events, and subscription events forwarded by Adapty. Network requests also expose connection information such as IP addresses to the receiving provider. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (Android, optional) — crash and reliability diagnostics when you enable “Crash reports.” Data can include installation identifiers, app and device versions, operating-system information, crash stack traces, and diagnostic metadata. Google Analytics is not enabled in this integration. See Firebase’s Privacy and Security information.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this public policy and support website and may process standard web request data under GitHub’s policies.

We do not intentionally send the content of your sermons, prayers, Scripture scans, notes, Bible passages or references, YouTube links, or AI questions to Adapty, AppsFlyer, Meta, or Firebase. HolyNote uses the same pseudonymous installation identifier as the Adapty customer identifier so our server can verify Premium access; the signed credential itself is not sent to these analytics providers. Providers may act as processors or independent controllers depending on their service and applicable terms.

7. Analytics, attribution, and tracking

HolyNote does not show third-party ads or sell personal data. The current iOS build may use AppsFlyer to measure installations and selected product or subscription events, but it does not access Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), enable Apple-defined cross-app tracking, or request permission through App Tracking Transparency (ATT). If a future version proposes to use IDFA or other Apple-defined tracking, we will update this policy and request permission before enabling it where required.

Android app measurement: the current Android build initializes the Meta SDK when the app enters the foreground. It reports installation and app activation, plus a limited set of commercial events such as onboarding completion, paywall views, plan selection, checkout outcomes, and restore outcomes. These client events do not contain your answers, Scripture, notes, prayers, or AI content. The current build has no in-app advertising-measurement switch and does not wait for a separate advertising opt-in.

Android acquisition attribution: AppsFlyer starts with the application and measures installations and sessions. HolyNote passes its pseudonymous app-instance identifier and a limited set of available campaign, ad-set, ad, source, and attribution-time fields to Adapty after the subscription profile is available. Android ID, IMEI, App Set ID, and advertising-identifier collection are disabled. This does not prevent the receiving provider from processing the IP address or deriving approximate location from it. The Android client sends no custom interaction or financial events to AppsFlyer; subscription lifecycle delivery to Meta continues through Adapty. AppsFlyer measurement does not depend on the separate optional crash-report setting.

Subscription measurement: the Android app passes the Facebook SDK’s “anonymous” app-device identifier to Adapty after SDK activation. Adapty can then associate subscription events with that identifier and forward configured trial, purchase, conversion, renewal, cancellation, expiry, billing, and refund events to Meta. This identifier is pseudonymous: its name does not mean that the related data cannot be associated with an app installation or combined with other information by a provider. Financial lifecycle events are forwarded by Adapty rather than duplicated by the Android client.

Android collection of the Google Advertising ID is disabled, and the app does not request the Advertising ID permission. Meta automatic purchase logging, codeless events, and advanced matching are disabled in the app. These restrictions do not eliminate the separate app-instance identifiers or event sharing described above.

Optional Android diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics remains disabled until you enable “Crash reports” in HolyNote’s Settings. You can turn it off there. This preference is independent of Meta measurement; turning it off does not disable subscription verification or Meta events.

8. Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes App Store payments on iOS, and Google Play processes purchases on Android. The relevant store manages your subscription account. We do not receive your full payment-card number. We and Adapty may receive purchase identifiers, product, price, currency, transaction timing, renewal, trial, refund, and subscription-status information so we can provide paid access, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and understand subscription performance. On Android, configured lifecycle and purchase information, including transaction identifiers, product, amount, and currency where applicable, is also shared with Meta for subscription and advertising measurement.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local content: remains on your device until you delete it, erase app data, or remove it through device management or backup controls.
  • Temporary media: is normally deleted from HolyNote’s processing server when the job succeeds, fails, or is cancelled. A system crash may delay cleanup until operational maintenance.
  • AI job transcripts and results: remain in the current server’s temporary in-memory job store for no more than 24 hours by default, or until the service restarts.
  • Provider data: is retained under the relevant provider’s policy, including OpenAI’s default abuse-monitoring period described above and Apple, Google Play, Adapty, AppsFlyer, Meta, or Firebase records used for purchases, attribution, diagnostics, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Retention varies by provider and purpose; uninstalling HolyNote does not automatically erase these records.
  • Security logs: are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for security, reliability, abuse prevention, dispute handling, and legal obligations.

Because HolyNote currently has no user account, we may not be able to associate an anonymous server job or provider profile with your identity after it expires. For a privacy request, provide only the minimum identifiers needed to locate relevant records. Do not email sermon audio, prayer text, or other sensitive content.
